# Tapstall — short answers

Each answer stands alone and quotes cleanly. Source of record: https://tapstall.whop.site

## What is Tapstall?

Tapstall is a store link for small shops: a customer taps it, picks from the menu and pays the exact amount, and the order arrives in a dashboard the shop owns.

## How much does Tapstall cost?

It is free for 60 days with no card. After that it is $7 a month or $60 a year, or R129 a month or R999 a year in South Africa.

## Which payments does Tapstall work with?

The shops it is built for use EcoCash, InnBucks, PayShap and ZIPIT, alongside ordinary bank transfers.

## Does Tapstall take a commission?

No. There is no order cap and no commission; the shop keeps its own order data and can export it to its own Google Sheet.

## How long does it take to open a store?

About 90 seconds: put the menu in, share the link, and start taking orders from the chat apps the shop already sells in.

## Does a customer need to install an app?

No. The store is a page behind a link, so it opens in whatever browser the customer already has.

## Why not just use Shopify?

Shopify and the platforms built for Silicon Valley assume cards, couriers and a checkout flow that many shops in Zimbabwe and South Africa do not have. Tapstall is built around local payment rails and chat-based selling.

## Who is Tapstall for?

Small retailers and social sellers in Zimbabwe and South Africa, especially those taking orders through WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram.
